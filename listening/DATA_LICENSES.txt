Modifications and mapping:
180 selected word WAVs are unmodified byte copies, renamed locally to opaque
sample IDs. Ten noise WAVs are one-second excerpts from seconds 0..5 of the
source white_noise.wav and pink_noise.wav, with fresh PCM16 WAV headers and
unchanged samples. These two source noises were generated synthetically, as
the original background README explains. data/selection_manifest.csv maps every
distributed input to its original path, hash, and any slice boundaries.

The .mmat template models in models/ are derived MFCC representations and
calibration parameters computed from this subset; they are not Google-provided
pretrained models. This distribution marks these audio-derived models and
associated source-derived selection metadata CC BY 4.0 as well. Preserve the
above attribution and modification notices when redistributing them.
